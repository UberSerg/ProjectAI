# Allocation Policy Research Notes

## Why Kraken may not buy stocks

If the expected equity premium over the CBR key-rate hurdle is missing or too small,
holding cash or (research) fixed income can be more rational than forcing equity exposure.

## Why a hurdle matters

Return without an alternative cost of capital is incomplete. The CBR key rate is the primary
ruble research hurdle — not a guaranteed deposit product.

## Why cash can be rational

Cash (relative to the hurdle) is a valid sleeve when opportunities are weak, data is
insufficient, or risk/liquidity constraints dominate. Empty cash is not a virtue.

## Why high yield ≠ good risk

A corporate bond yield is not a guaranteed return. Unknown credit quality remains unsafe for
real portfolios even when cashflow accounting looks correct.
