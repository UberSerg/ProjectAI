# Portfolio Risk Gate V0

Protective layer before a 100 000 ₽ research portfolio candidate:

```text
Opportunity → Risk Checks → Eligibility → Portfolio Candidate
```

## Statuses

- `APPROVED`
- `APPROVED_WITH_WARNINGS`
- `RESEARCH_ONLY`
- `BLOCKED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`

## Principle

Yield alone never approves. Credit UNKNOWN, low liquidity, incomplete data, or
concentration breaches change admission — without inventing SAFE labels.
